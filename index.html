Global Economic Drivers: Trade, Investment, and Growth
This dashboard allows an exploratory analysis of global economic trends, specifically the interactions of GDP growth and various other macroeconomic variables.
Click on the map or use the filters to select a country, the plots below show key metrics.
The color on the map indicates movement of wealth. All countries are ranked by their annual GDP and then binned. The darker colors equal higher GDP. Use the slider below the map to see changes over the years.

Hover over a country to see the GDP value, and click on in to see key metrics below.

For countries colored gray no data is available for the selected year.
This chart shows annual absolute changes of GDP for a country. This way, the growth path becomes visible and each country's trajectory over time can be seen in detail.
By plotting Foreign Direct Investment (attractiveness to capital) against Trade (reliance on exchange), this chart reveals distinct economic archetypes.

Including GDP Purchasing Power Parity as bubble size adds a layer of reality, showing actual economic weight versus relative percentages.
This chart allows to compare whether a country relies heavily on import or export or has a trading balance.

By giving the context of countries on the same continent comparison of economic strength is possible.
